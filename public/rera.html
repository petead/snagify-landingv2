What the law actually says

The authority behind every Dubai rental

RERA — the Real Estate Regulatory Agency, an arm of the Dubai Land Department — regulates landlord-tenant relationships in Dubai. When a conflict arises, the Rental Dispute Center (RDC) settles it.

Here is what surprises most tenants and landlords: Dubai's tenancy law (Law 26 of 2007, as amended) does not require a condition report at handover. No mandatory check-in form, no official template. Which means that when a deposit dispute reaches the RDC, there is no default document in the file — the case rests on whatever evidence the parties can produce. A signed, dated, photo-backed condition report is the strongest evidence a party can bring. Without one, it is word against word.

Common questions

On RERA, the RDC, and how Snagify fits into Dubai's rental framework.

Is a Snagify report officially accepted by the RDC?
No format is pre-approved by the RDC — no software can truthfully claim an official stamp. What the RDC weighs is the quality of the evidence: identification, dates, signatures from both parties, photographic backing, and integrity. Snagify reports are engineered on those exact criteria, which is what "RDC-ready" means.
What if only one party signs the report?
The report keeps its evidential value for the party who signed — and Snagify documents the other side's response. A refusal is recorded with its timestamp and reason. A party who simply never responds is documented too: the report prints when their signature was requested, whether they viewed it, when reminders were sent, and that the signing period lapsed. Silence becomes part of the record instead of a hole in it.
Does the format matter?
Yes. Handwritten forms, WhatsApp photos and editable Word files are routinely challenged as altered or incomplete. A hash-verified PDF with dual signatures and a verification page is materially harder to dispute.
Do I need Snagify for both check-in AND check-out?
Ideally yes: the check-out report cites its check-in by report ID and hash, forming a verifiable chain, and every finding is compared against the signed baseline. That said, a Snagify check-out alongside an older paper check-in still adds significant weight — the comparison is just manual.
Is the check-in really free?
Yes, always, for individual landlords and tenants. Credits are only consumed for check-out inspections. Professional plans include a monthly credit allocation.
How long should I keep my inspection reports?
Keep them for the life of the tenancy and well beyond it — limitation periods for tenancy claims vary by claim type. Snagify retains signed reports and their verification hashes for at least five years, including after account closure, so every signing party can verify their document.
